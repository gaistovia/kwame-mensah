02Who Am I

The mindset

I don't design interfaces.
I design the moment someone decides to trust you.

I grew up in Kumasi watching my mother run a small textile stall — pricing by instinct, reading a customer's hesitation before they said a word. That's still the job. Every product I touch is an attempt to remove hesitation.

Eight years ago I was the only designer at a Lagos fintech startup, sketching wireframes on the back of receipt paper because we couldn't afford software licenses. Today I advise product teams from Nairobi to New York — but the instinct hasn't changed: watch people closely, build only what earns their confidence, and cut everything else.

My mission is simple. Make African-built products the default standard other markets measure themselves against — not the exception that needs explaining.

Clarity over cleverness
Evidence over opinion
Craft without ego
Accra, Ghana — between meetings
03The Journey

A timeline, not a résumé

Eight years. Four cities. One throughline.

  1. 2016

    The receipt-paper wireframes

    Joined a three-person fintech startup in Lagos as its first product designer. No budget, no playbook — just users who needed to trust an app with their salary.

  2. 2018

    First product that shipped at scale

    Led design on a savings product that grew to 400,000 users in eighteen months, proving the thesis: trust-first design outperforms feature-first design in emerging markets.

  3. 2020

    Built my first design system from scratch

    Codified three years of scattered decisions into a documented system — cutting a growing team's shipping time by 40% and ending the "which button style is correct" debates for good.

  4. 2022

    Went independent

    Left full-time work to advise founders directly — from pre-seed teams validating an idea to Series B companies rebuilding for scale.

  5. 2024

    Crossed three continents

    Active client base expanded to teams in Ghana, Kenya, the UK and the US — all working from the same underlying playbook.

  6. 2025

    Today

    Working with a small, deliberately limited roster of founders and executive teams who are done guessing and ready to build with evidence.

06Process

How the work actually happens

Five stages. No shortcuts.

Research

Sit inside the problem before touching a single screen — customer interviews, support tickets, funnel data, competitor teardown.

Strategy

Turn findings into a small number of hard decisions the team can commit to, written down where everyone can hold each other to them.

Execution

Design and prototype in the open, in short cycles, with engineering in the room from day one — not handed a spec at the end.

Delivery

Ship in slices small enough to learn from, with a rollback plan and a measurement plan agreed before the first line of code.

Growth

Watch what real users actually do, then come back and cut, sharpen or double down — the work is never really finished.
